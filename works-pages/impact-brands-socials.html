Impact Brands

Social Media Visual Strategy

- 2023 -

About the project

To align with the refreshed Impact Brands brandbook, I developed a bold, minimal, and professional social media visual strategy. This approach not only reflects the company’s youthful and modern identity but also positions it as an attractive workplace for tech-savvy and creative talents. The strategy balances impactful visuals, authentic imagery, and practical templates, creating a cohesive and engaging presence across platforms.

Process Overview

  • Goal

    To design a modern, youthful social media style that attracts top talent while maintaining Impact Brands’ bold and recognizable identity.

  • Concept

    Adopt a bold minimal style that retains IB’s signature colors and fonts. Shift towards a more conceptual, tech-inspired aesthetic to appeal to creative and innovative professionals. Avoid overly corporate visuals.

  • Visual System

    Created a flexible visual system rather than rigid templates, allowing for variety while maintaining a cohesive look. Mixed and matched design elements to ensure the feed remains visually dynamic and engaging.

  • Visual Harmony

    Only authentic images with open spaces, primarily outdoor or "impactor" photos, avoiding stock whenever possible. Maintaining a balance between photographs and graphics to keep the feed interesting and visually appealing.

  • Content Strategy

    Share Impact Brands’ achievements and daily life to build an authentic narrative. Provide valuable information for young professionals, emphasizing IB as a workplace for growth and development.
