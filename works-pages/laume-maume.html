UI/UX Case Study:
Candle E-Shop

A Comprehensive Approach to User Experience Design

- 2020 -

About the Candle E-Shop Project

This project is the culmination of my UI/UX design course, where I applied a full end-to-end design process to create a functional and visually appealing candle e-shop. By combining user research, thoughtful design, and interactive elements, I demonstrated my ability to craft user-centric digital experiences.

Moodboards

Setting the Visual Tone

I started with moodboards to establish the aesthetic direction of the e-shop, combining colors, textures, and imagery that evoke warmth, elegance, and simplicity to reflect the candle brand’s identity.

Understanding the Audience

I created detailed personas to represent the target users, focusing on their needs, goals, and behaviors. These personas guided design decisions to ensure the e-shop would meet user expectations effectively. As the e-shop is created in Lithuanian they are in Lithuanian language to understant local customers better.

User Interview Questions

Validating User Needs

Developed targeted interview questions to uncover user preferences, shopping habits, and challenges. These insights formed the foundation for designing a user-friendly e-commerce experience. Adding short example of the questionnaire.

Sitemap

Structuring the Website

Designed a clear and intuitive sitemap to organize the e-shop’s content, ensuring effortless navigation and a logical flow for users.

Low-Fidelity Wireframes

Visualizing the Layout

Sketched initial wireframes to define the structure and placement of key elements, focusing on functionality and usability.

Brandbook

Building Brand Identity

Developed a brandbook outlining the visual guidelines, including logo, typography, and color palette, to maintain consistency across the e-shop.

High-Fidelity Wireframes

Refining the Design

Created detailed wireframes with precise layouts, colors, and typography to bridge the gap between concept and final design.

Final Design, Prototype and Web Animations

Bringing the E-Shop to Life

Designed the final e-shop interface with smooth web animations and interactive elements, enhancing user engagement and elevating the shopping experience.
